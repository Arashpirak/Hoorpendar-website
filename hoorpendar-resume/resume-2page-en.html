About us

Since 1999, Hoorpendar has been engineering and manufacturing industrial electronic and control systems for critical infrastructure and process industries, with expertise spanning power electronics, instrumentation, automation and industrial software. Our engineering and manufacturing capabilities are vertically integrated, covering electronic and firmware design, PCB assembly, testing, calibration and Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT). Our systems can therefore be engineered and customized to meet project-specific requirements, including supply voltage, mechanical dimensions, I/O configuration, communication interfaces and applicable standards.

Featured products

Alarm annunciator

PAL750 · PAL625 · SAL-82

Modular and programmable, it supports 2 to 512 alarm points with ISA-18.1 sequences. It offers Modbus communication, first-out indication and high-brightness LED windows.

Pressure-to-current (P/I) converter

PI-315 · PIT-315N

Converts a 3–15 psi pneumatic signal to a 4–20 mA output, with zero and span adjustment and a pressure gauge. It suits upgrades of pneumatic instrumentation.

Gas turbine flame detector

FDU-300 · FDU-320 · FR-430E

Detects flame in gas turbines, boilers and burners with a UV sensor, and trips on flame loss. More than 200 units are in operation.

Dam reservoir level meter

HP-LVLM100 · BLVM-100

Measures water level in dam reservoirs and canals with a digital float or an air-purge (bubbler) system, and sends it to the control room and data logger over analogue and digital outputs.

Also available: SECON-2000 static excitation system and DHTG-300 digital hydro-turbine governor · IGRU-200 high-energy ignition unit and 18 kV ignition exciters · SBC-600/620 smart battery charger controllers and SMART-128 charger · FMS-280 non-contact flare monitoring · MCI-870 8-channel process monitor · RUG-280 ramp generator and fuel valve control · STB-2408S voltage stabiliser · HIPPS · master clock systems and digital frequency meters.
