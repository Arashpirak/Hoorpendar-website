Hoorpendar Computer Technology Industries · Knowledge-based company · Est. 1999

We design and manufacture industrial electronics and deliver control, automation and instrumentation projects for oil and gas, water, power and heavy industry. We take each project from engineering study and design through commissioning and long-term support.

About us

Since 1999, Hoorpendar’s engineers have worked in power electronics, control systems and industrial software. Every stage happens in-house, from circuit and firmware design to PCB assembly, factory acceptance testing and calibration. That is why most products can be built to a project’s supply voltage, dimensions, output type and standard, and why equipment that has lost manufacturer support can be rebuilt or redesigned. The head office is in Tehran and the production workshop is in the Payam Airport Special Economic Zone.

Featured products

Alarm annunciator

PAL750 · PAL625 · SAL-82

Modular and programmable, it handles 2 to 512 alarm points with ISA-18.1 sequences. It offers Modbus communication, first-out indication and high-brightness LED windows.

Pressure-to-current (P/I) converter

PI-315 · PIT-315N

Converts a 3–15 psi pneumatic signal to a 4–20 mA output, with zero and span adjustment and a pressure gauge. It suits upgrades of pneumatic instrumentation.

Gas turbine flame detector

FDU-300 · FDU-320 · FR-430E

Detects flame in gas turbines, boilers and burners with a UV sensor, and trips on flame loss. More than 200 units are in service.

Dam reservoir level meter

HP-LVLM100 · BLVM-100

Measures water level in dam reservoirs and canals with a digital float or an air-purge (bubbler) system, and sends it to the control room and data logger over analogue and digital outputs.

Also available: SECON-2000 static excitation system and DHTG-300 digital hydro-turbine governor · IGRU-200 high-energy ignition unit and 18 kV ignition exciters · SBC-600/620 smart battery charger controllers and SMART-128 charger · FMS-280 non-contact flare monitoring · MCI-870 8-channel process monitor · RUG-280 ramp generator and fuel valve control · STB-2408S voltage stabiliser · HIPPS · master clock systems and digital frequency meters.
